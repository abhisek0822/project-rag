# Northstar Employee Handbook

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests longer than three consecutive working days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Unused annual leave may carry forward, but the balance may not exceed 24 days.

## Casual leave

Employees receive 12 casual leave days per calendar year. Casual leave is intended for short, unexpected absences and does not carry forward into the following year.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days each week with their manager's approval. Tuesday is the company-wide collaboration day, when employees assigned to an office are expected to attend in person unless they have an approved exception.

## Learning allowance

Each employee has an annual learning allowance of INR 30,000. The allowance may be used for courses, books, professional certifications, and conferences related to the employee's role. Manager approval is required before purchase.

## Information security

Confidential company data must be stored only in approved systems. Suspected data loss or unauthorized access must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery.
